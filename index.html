Frequently asked questions

Is this an official KIIT service?

No — this is a student-built directory to help peers find each other for hostel swaps. All actual hostel changes must still be approved through official KIIT hostel administration channels.

Is my data shared with anyone?

Your details are only shown to other students browsing the swap directory so they can contact you. We don't sell or share your data with third parties.

Can I update or remove my listing later?

Yes. Once connected to the backend, you'll be able to edit or delete your submission using your registered account at any time.

What if no one matches my swap?

Use the "Notify Me When Someone Matches" button on the results page and we'll alert you the moment a matching student joins.

About the portal

KIIT Hostel Swap Portal was built to solve a simple, recurring problem — students wanting to swap hostel blocks with no easy way to find each other. Instead of relying on scattered WhatsApp groups and notice boards, this portal brings every swap request into one clean, searchable place.
